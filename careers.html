Media & Data Analyst
Full-timeGaborone

The quality layer sitting between our automated data engine and our clients — QA the automated media feed, capture radio and TV mentions our providers miss, and compile the reports clients rely on daily.

Details

About the role

MediaClip tracks print, radio, TV, online and social media across Botswana, with Setswana-language sentiment built into the pipeline. You'll be the quality layer sitting between the automated data engine and our clients, making sure every report that goes out is accurate, complete and genuinely useful.

What you'll do

  • QA the automated media feed daily, catching gaps, mis-tags and sentiment errors before clients see them
  • Capture and log radio and TV mentions not covered by our automated data providers, using station recordings and monitoring schedules
  • Compile and quality-check client reports and alerts on a daily, weekly and monthly cadence
  • Correct false positives and duplicate mentions, and apply consistent classification rules across clients
  • Maintain each client's keyword list, competitor set and the master source list as Botswana's media landscape shifts
  • Escalate critical or reputationally sensitive mentions within agreed response times
  • Document recurring corrections to help improve the automated classification engine over time

Minimum qualification

Diploma or degree in Journalism, Media Studies, Communications, PR, Research, Data Analytics or a related field, or equivalent demonstrable experience.

Minimum experience

2 to 3 years in media monitoring, journalism, research, PR reporting or communications analysis.

Essential competencies

  • Fluent in Setswana and English, written and spoken
  • Strong report-writing, proofreading and summarising ability
  • Proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets
  • Comfortable interpreting dashboards, trends and basic data visualisations
  • Able to distinguish fact, opinion, sentiment and reputational risk in coverage

Desirable

  • Prior use of media monitoring or social listening software
  • Broadcast (radio/TV) monitoring experience

What to submit

  • A short writing or analysis sample
  • An example report, article, media summary or research output
  • Two contactable professional references

Employment terms

  • Based in Gaborone; standard office hours, with occasional early or weekend monitoring
  • 3-month probation period
  • Must be eligible to work in Botswana
  • Shortlisted candidates complete a practical assessment: classify a small set of media mentions, identify errors, and prepare a one-page executive summary
