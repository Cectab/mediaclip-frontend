Accounting hand-off checklist

  1. Create the client in QuickBooks with the same registered name and address as the signed quote (top-right of the quote header). Attach the countersigned quote PDF to the customer record.
  2. Under Products & Services, confirm every item code above exists. First time only — after that they are reused across every client.
  3. Build a Recurring Sales Receipt / Invoice schedule for the subscription line(s). Frequency: monthly. Start date: contract start date on the quote. End date: 12 months later or the term stated on the quote.
  4. Create a separate one-off invoice for the onboarding line and any bespoke reports scheduled in month 1. Terms: 30 days from invoice date.
  5. Set the client's payment terms to Net 30. Attach the DPA and Service Agreement to the customer record.
  6. Record the contract start date, end date and renewal window in a QuickBooks memo on the customer record — so the auto-renewal 60-day notice window is easy to find later.
  7. If VAT-registered under BURS, apply 14% VAT at invoice time. The grand total on the quote is VAT-inclusive; QuickBooks will handle the split automatically once the items are marked as VAT-applicable.
