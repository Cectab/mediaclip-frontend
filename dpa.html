Data Processing Agreement.

This Data Processing Agreement forms part of the MediaClip Terms of Service. It sets out how MediaClip, as data Processor, handles personal information on behalf of the Client, who acts as data Controller. Written to satisfy the requirements of the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2018, and give the Client's legal team the answers they will look for.

Regulator: Information & Data Protection Commission

01Parties and background

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between:

  • MediaClip ("Processor", "we", "us"), registered in the Republic of Botswana; and
  • The client organisation named on the applicable order form or Subscription ("Controller", "Client").

This DPA forms part of the Terms of Service and governs the processing of personal information by MediaClip on behalf of the Client in connection with the Client's use of the MediaClip platform. It is issued in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2018 of the Republic of Botswana.

02Definitions

Personal Information
Has the meaning given in the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2018 — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
Processing
Any operation performed on Personal Information, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, and deletion.
Data Subject
The natural person to whom Personal Information relates.
Sub-processor
A third party engaged by MediaClip to process Personal Information on the Client's behalf.
Client Data
All data, including Personal Information, that the Client submits to, or has collected on its behalf through, the MediaClip platform.
Security Incident
Any unauthorised or accidental access to, disclosure of, alteration of, loss of, or destruction of Personal Information.

03Roles and scope of processing

For the purposes of this DPA:

  • The Client is the Controller of Client Data. The Client determines the purposes and means of processing.
  • MediaClip is the Processor, acting on the Client's documented instructions.
  • The processing carried out by MediaClip on behalf of the Client is described in the annexes to this DPA and includes: media coverage ingestion, storage, indexing, sentiment scoring, dashboard rendering, reporting, and export.
  • The duration of processing is the term of the Client's Subscription, plus a limited post-termination period as set out below.
  • The categories of Data Subjects include: individuals mentioned in publicly available media coverage relevant to the Client, and the Client's Authorised Users.

04Controller obligations

The Client warrants that:

  • It has a lawful basis under applicable law for the processing of Personal Information it instructs MediaClip to perform.
  • Its instructions do not require MediaClip to process Personal Information in a manner that would breach the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2018 or any other applicable law.
  • It has provided all necessary notices to, and where required, obtained consent from, Data Subjects for the processing carried out through the Platform.
  • It will not upload or provide Client Data that contains categories of Personal Information the platform is not designed to handle, including sensitive personal information as defined by the Act, unless expressly agreed in writing.

05Processor obligations

MediaClip shall:

  • Process Personal Information only on the Client's documented instructions, including as set out in these Terms, this DPA, and the Client's use of the Platform's features.
  • Ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Information are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Implement the technical and organisational security measures set out in this DPA.
  • Assist the Client in responding to Data Subject requests and in demonstrating compliance with applicable data-protection law.
  • Not use Personal Information for its own purposes or for the purposes of any other client.

06Multi-tenant isolation

MediaClip operates the Platform as a multi-tenant service. Client Data is logically isolated from every other client's data by a client identifier enforced at the database query layer. No Authorised User of any other client is able to access, view, or infer the Client's Data through any feature of the Platform.

What is shared across clients: only the pool of publicly ingested media coverage. Where two clients both configure the platform to track the same public brand, story, or keyword, both may see the same public article — because that article is public. No client-specific configuration, note, tag, workflow, report, or user activity is ever shared, disclosed, or otherwise made accessible to another client.

07Sub-processing

The Client authorises MediaClip to engage third-party sub-processors to carry out limited processing activities on MediaClip's behalf. A current list of sub-processors will be provided directly to the Client on request, together with a summary of each sub-processor's function and location. Each sub-processor is bound by contractual obligations no less protective than those set out in this DPA.

MediaClip will notify the Client at least 30 days in advance of any material change to its sub-processor list. The Client may object in writing on reasonable grounds; where objections cannot be resolved, the Client may terminate the affected portion of the Subscription without penalty.

08Data security measures

MediaClip implements and maintains appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including:

  • Encryption in transit — TLS 1.2 or higher for all data transmitted between the Client and the Platform, and between the Platform and its sub-processors.
  • Encryption at rest — Client Data at rest is encrypted using industry-standard algorithms.
  • Access controls — role-based access, principle of least privilege, and multi-factor authentication for administrative access to the underlying infrastructure.
  • Password storage — Authorised User passwords are stored as salted bcrypt hashes; the plaintext value is not retained.
  • Audit logging — access to sensitive endpoints and administrative operations is logged and retained.
  • Vulnerability management — dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities and patched on a defined schedule.
  • Backups — daily encrypted backups of the database, retained for 30 days.
  • Personnel — team members with access to Personal Information receive training on data-protection obligations and are subject to confidentiality undertakings.

09Security incident notification

MediaClip will notify the Client without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting the Client's Personal Information. The notification will include:

  • A description of the nature of the incident, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;
  • The likely consequences;
  • The measures taken or proposed to address the incident and mitigate its possible adverse effects; and
  • The name and contact details of MediaClip's Data Protection Officer.

The Client is responsible for any further notification to Data Subjects or the Information & Data Protection Commission of Botswana, with MediaClip's reasonable co-operation.

10Data Subject rights assistance

MediaClip will assist the Client, insofar as reasonably possible, in responding to requests from Data Subjects to exercise their rights under the Data Protection Act, 2018, including rights of access, correction, erasure, and portability. Where a Data Subject contacts MediaClip directly with such a request in relation to Client Data, MediaClip will refer them to the Client and notify the Client of the request.

11Location of processing and cross-border transfers

Client Data is stored primarily in the European Union. Certain third-party service providers are located outside Botswana, including in the European Union and the United States. Where Personal Information is transferred across borders, MediaClip ensures that appropriate safeguards are in place, including contractual data-protection commitments consistent with the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2018 and, where applicable, standards of the receiving jurisdiction.

12Return or deletion of data on termination

On termination of the Subscription:

  • Client Data will remain available for the Client to export via the Platform for 30 days after termination.
  • After the 30-day export window, MediaClip will delete all Client Data, including from operational backups within a further 30 days.
  • On written request during the export window, MediaClip will provide a bulk export of Client Data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • MediaClip may retain minimal records where required by law (for example, financial and tax records), for the shortest period permitted, and continues to apply this DPA's protections to those records for as long as they are retained.

13Audit

The Client, or an independent auditor mandated by the Client and reasonably acceptable to MediaClip, may audit MediaClip's compliance with this DPA once per calendar year, on reasonable prior written notice, during business hours, and subject to reasonable confidentiality undertakings. MediaClip may satisfy this obligation by providing recent third-party audit reports, security certifications, or written responses to a documented audit questionnaire.

14Liability and indemnity

The liability of each party under this DPA is subject to, and forms part of, the aggregate liability limits set out in the Terms of Service, except where otherwise required by mandatory provisions of the Data Protection Act, 2018.

15Governing law and dispute resolution

This DPA is governed by the laws of the Republic of Botswana. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this DPA shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Botswana. The parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute in good faith through senior-executive escalation before commencing formal proceedings.

16Order of precedence

In the event of conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Service or any order form, this DPA prevails on matters of Personal Information processing. Otherwise, the order of precedence is: (1) order form, (2) Terms of Service, (3) this DPA, (4) the Privacy Policy.
