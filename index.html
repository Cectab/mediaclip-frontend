FREQUENTLY ASKED

Things people ask before they sign.

Relevant print, radio, broadcast television, digital news portals, and public social conversations across supported networks. The specific source list is confirmed during onboarding and depends on outlet availability, licensing terms and platform API policies. Update frequency and coverage windows are disclosed per source in your service documentation.
Every mention is auto-classified by our Signal Engine against a four-way intent taxonomy — Risk, Purchase, Cancel, Service (RPCS) — plus sentiment (positive / negative / neutral) and sentiment type (operational vs reputational). Threshold alerts route each signal to the right desk.
Set thresholds on volume, sentiment, source, or intent. When those thresholds are crossed, MediaClip notifies your team via email and the dashboard — routed by severity and by team. Each alert carries the source clip and the classification reason. WhatsApp and Microsoft Teams delivery are planned for a future release.
Client data is handled in line with Botswana's Data Protection Act 2024 (Act 18 of 2024). Access is role-based, transmission is encrypted, and every enterprise engagement is covered by a Data Processing Agreement specifying retention, subcontractors, incident response and account deletion. Data residency options are configurable per client. Full security documentation is available on request.
Most clients see their first mentions in the dashboard within 48 hours of the onboarding call. A full working dashboard — with alerts routed and the first weekly report scheduled — is typically live within the first week. Historic backfill on request.
We offer a free five-day brand snapshot instead of a generic software trial: we run monitoring on your brand across supported channels for five days, then share the findings with an analyst walk-through. It's a better test of value than a self-service trial, and it costs you nothing to try.
